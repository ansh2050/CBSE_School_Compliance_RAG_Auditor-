# CBSE Academic Subjects Structure

## Mandatory and Optional Subject Framework (Classes IX and X)
- Compulsory Core Subjects (5 Subjects):
  - Subject 1: Language I (Hindi Course-A / Hindi Course-B / English Language and Literature / English Communicative) [Group-L]
  - Subject 2: Language II (Any one language from Group-L other than Language chosen as Subject 1) [Group-L]
  - Subject 3: Mathematics Standard (Code 041) or Mathematics Basic (Code 241) / Mathematics Advanced [Group-A1]
  - Subject 4: Science [Group-A1]
  - Subject 5: Social Science [Group-A1]
- Optional Subjects (Up to 2 Additional Subjects):
  - Subject 6: Skill Subject [Group-S] or Academic Elective [Group-A2]
  - Subject 7: Language III [Group-L] or Academic Elective [Group-A2]
- Compulsory Internal Assessment Subjects (School-Level Certification):
  - Subject 8: Art Education
  - Subject 9: Health and Physical Education (Work Experience is subsumed under Health and Physical Education)
- Subject Combination Restrictions:
  - Out of Computer Application (Code 165), Information Technology (Code 402), and Artificial Intelligence (Code 417), only ONE subject can be offered. Combination of any of these subjects is strictly prohibited.
- Provisions for Children with Special Needs (CWSN):
  - Candidates with benchmark disabilities have the option of opting for 1 compulsory language instead of 2 or 3, and a maximum of 2 skill-based subjects from Group-S.

## Mandatory and Optional Subject Framework (Classes XI and XII)
- Compulsory Subject:
  - Subject 1: Language I (English Core / English Elective / Hindi Core / Hindi Elective) [Group-L]
- Elective Subjects (4 Subjects):
  - Subjects 2, 3, 4, and 5: Any 4 subjects chosen from Academic Electives [Group-A] OR Skill Subjects [Group-S] OR a combination of Group-A and Group-S.
- Optional Additional Subject (6th Subject):
  - Subject 6: Any one subject from Group-A or Group-S not opted above.
- Compulsory Co-Scholastic & Internal Assessment Areas (Subjects 7 to 9):
  - Code 500: General Studies
  - Code 501: Work Experience
  - Code 502: Health and Physical Education

## Structural Differences Between Class 9-10 and Class 11-12
- Class 9-10 Course Architecture: Composite 2-year course structure; mandates 5 core subjects across 2 languages, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science, supplemented by 1-2 skill/language optionals.
- Class 11-12 Course Architecture: Specialized stream-based structure; mandates 1 language + 4 core electives (Academic or Skill), with 1 optional 6th subject.
- Level Disambiguation: Class 10 offers dual-level Mathematics (Basic vs. Standard) and Science (Standard vs. Advanced); Class 11-12 offers specialized domain disciplines (Physics, Chemistry, Accountancy, Political Science, etc.).

## Weekly Period and Instructional Time Allocation
- Standard Period Duration: 40 minutes per instructional period.
- First Language (Language I / R1): 7 periods per week.
- Second Language (Language II / R2): 6 periods per week.
- Third Language (Language III / R3): 5 to 6 periods per week (logged under NCFSE transition roadmaps).
- Mathematics: 6 to 7 periods per week.
- Science: 6 periods per week (integrated theory and practical instruction).
- Social Science: 6 periods per week (covering History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics).
- Health and Physical Education (HPE): Minimum 200 periods per academic year (approx. 4 to 5 periods per week), comprising Games/Sports (90 periods), Health/Fitness (50 periods), and SEWA projects.
- Art Education: 2 to 3 periods per week.
- Environmental Education: Minimum 2 periods per week.
- Life Skills Education: Minimum 2 periods per week.
- Skill / Vocational Subjects: 12 to 15 instructional hours per skill module (35 marks practical / 15 marks theory split for 50-mark papers).
