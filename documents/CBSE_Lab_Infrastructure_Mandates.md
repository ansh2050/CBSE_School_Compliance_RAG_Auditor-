# CBSE Lab Infrastructure Mandates

## Practical Hours and Instructional Time
- Science Practicals (Classes IX-X): Integrated within the weekly 6-period allocation for Science.
- Senior Secondary Science Practicals (Physics, Chemistry, Biology - Classes XI-XII): Minimum 2 to 3 dedicated practical periods per week per science subject.
- Computer / IT Practicals: Minimum 2 dedicated practical periods per week.
- Skill Subjects Practical Allocation: Minimum 15 instructional hours dedicated to hands-on practical activities per skill module.

## Spatial Footprint and Room Size Minimums
- Composite Science Laboratory (Secondary Classes IX-X): Minimum 600 sq. ft. (9 m × 6 m / 54 sq. m).
- Physics Laboratory (Senior Secondary Classes XI-XII): Minimum 600 sq. ft. (9 m × 6 m / 54 sq. m).
- Chemistry Laboratory (Senior Secondary Classes XI-XII): Minimum 600 sq. ft. (9 m × 6 m / 54 sq. m).
- Biology Laboratory (Senior Secondary Classes XI-XII): Minimum 600 sq. ft. (9 m × 6 m / 54 sq. m).
- Computer Laboratory / IT Laboratory: Minimum 600 sq. ft. (9 m × 6 m / 54 sq. m).
- Mathematics Laboratory: Minimum size of a regular classroom, i.e., 500 sq. ft. (8 m × 6 m / 48 sq. m).
- Central School Library: Minimum 1200 sq. ft. (14 m × 8 m / 112 sq. m). Accommodates at least 50 students at a time plus 10% extra space for group activities and reference desks.
- Branch School Urban Site Library Threshold: Minimum 8 m × 8 m (64 sq. m / approx. 688 sq. ft.).

## Hard Equipment and Physical Setup Requirements
- Science Laboratories (Physics, Chemistry, Biology):
  - Demonstration Table: Minimum 1 demonstration table equipped with running water tap and sink.
  - Student Seating Capacity: Minimum 40 lab stools/seating arrangements.
  - Chemistry Benches and Sinks: Chemical-resistant laboratory counter surfaces; minimum 8 sinks with continuous water supply.
  - Gas Pipeline System: Dedicated gas pipeline system with minimum 2 heating burners.
  - Lockable Storage: Separate lockable cupboards/storerooms for hazardous consumables and non-consumable equipment.
  - Safety Infrastructure: Display boards detailing laboratory Do's & Don'ts, emergency contact numbers, fire extinguishers, and eye-wash safety points.
- Computer / IT Laboratory Infrastructure:
  - Centralized Server: Minimum Core i5/i7 CPU, 16 GB RAM, 2 TB × 4 storage running Windows Server 2016/2019 or Linux.
  - Workstation Hardware Specs: Intel Core i3 processor or higher, 8 GB RAM, 500 GB Hard Drive, 15.6-inch display, dedicated UPS backup.
  - Network & Connectivity: High-speed 1:1 Leased Line / Wi-Fi internet connection with minimum 20 Mbps bandwidth; Enterprise Firewall protection and Centralized LAN.
  - Peripherals & Display: Minimum 2 printers and 2 scanners (Auto Document Feeder / ADF) per computer lab; Interactive Smart Board with digital projector and audio speakers.

## Computer-to-Student Ratios
- General Computer Laboratory Ratio: Minimum 1 computer for every 20 students (revised digital norm: 1 computer system per 18 students).
- Absolute Minimum Computer Nodes: Minimum 20 functional computer nodes for basic affiliation; minimum 40 computer nodes for schools exceeding 800 enrolled students.
- Multimedia / Specialized Studio Ratio: 1:1 compulsory computer-to-student ratio.
- Vocational / Mass Media Skill Ratio: Minimum 1 computer for every 5 students.

## Library Book Counts, Periodical Mandates, and Librarian Qualifications
- Minimum Book Collection: Minimum 1,500 books total in the library collection.
- Per-Student Scaling Threshold: Minimum 5 books per child for schools with total enrollment exceeding 300 students.
- Periodicals & Magazines Mandate: Minimum 10 active magazine subscriptions.
- Daily Newspaper Mandate: Minimum 6 daily newspapers (including 2 English, 2 Hindi, and 2 regional language newspapers).
- Automation and OPAC Norms: Computerized barcoded circulation system; OPAC access terminals provided in a ratio of 1 terminal for every 5 students attending the library simultaneously.
- Librarian Qualifications (TGT / PGT Grade - 1 Mandated Post):
  - Master's Degree in Arts/Science/Commerce PLUS Degree or equivalent diploma in Library & Information Science from a recognized University; OR Master's Degree in Library & Information Science (M.Lib.I.Sc.).
- Assistant Librarian Qualifications (PRT Grade - 1 Post):
  - Degree or Diploma in Library & Information Science from a recognized University.
