# CBSE Fees Admissions Operational Compliance

## School Fees and Fee Hike Regulations
- Non-Profit Motive Mandate: Societies, Trusts, or Section 8 Companies running affiliated schools must operate without profit motive (Affiliation Bye-Laws Chapter 7, Rule 7.1).
- Fee Commensurability: Fees charged from students must be strictly commensurate with the physical facilities, staff infrastructure, and educational amenities provided by the school (Rule 11.1).
- Fee Heads Authorization: Fees must be charged only under fee heads explicitly prescribed by the Department of Education of the Appropriate State / Union Territory Government.
- Prohibition of Arbitrary Fee Hikes: Unilateral or arbitrary fee increases during an academic session are strictly prohibited. Any fee structure revision must be submitted and approved in alignment with State Education Department norms and School Management Committee (SMC) resolutions prior to the academic session.

## Prohibition of Capitation Fees and Donations
- Strict Capitation Ban (Rule 7.1 & Rule 11.1): No Society, Trust, Company, or School shall charge capitation fee or accept voluntary/forced donations directly or indirectly for student admissions or any other institutional purpose.
- Prohibition of Screening Procedures: Schools are strictly prohibited from subjecting children or parents/guardians to any screening test, interview, or profiling during the admission process, adhering strictly to RTE Act Section 13 and CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws.

## Prohibition of Post-Dated Cheques (PDCs)
- PDC Collection Ban: Schools are strictly prohibited from demanding, collecting, or retaining Post-Dated Cheques (PDCs) from parents as a precondition for admission, fee payment, or enrollment retention.
- Authorized Fee Payment Schedule: Fees must be collected periodically (monthly or quarterly) via transparent electronic payment gateways, direct bank transfers, or demand drafts.

## Prohibition on Retaining Original Transfer Certificates and Mark Sheets
- Prompt TC Issuance Rule: Schools cannot withhold, deny, or retain original Transfer Certificates (TCs), Birth Certificates, or Board Mark Sheets of students under any financial dispute, unpaid fee dispute, or administrative pretext.
- Mandatory TC Processing: Transfer Certificates must be issued promptly upon formal parent application within statutory timelines defined under State RTE rules and CBSE guidelines.

## Teacher-to-Student Ratios and Classroom Dimensions
- Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR): Mandated baseline PTR across secondary and senior secondary classes is 1:30 (1 teacher for every 30 students). Overall teacher staffing must maintain 1.5 teachers per classroom section.
- Classroom Dimensional Minimums: Minimum classroom spatial size must be 500 sq. ft. (8 m × 6 m / 48 sq. m).
- Section Enrollment Cap: Student strength per classroom section is strictly capped at a maximum of 40 students.

## Mandatory Annual Declarations and Public Website Disclosures
- Mandatory Public Disclosure Threshold (Rule 14.5 / SARAS Portal): Every affiliated school must maintain a dedicated 'Mandatory Public Disclosure' page on its official website, updated annually before 15th September.
- Mandatory Published Documents and Information:
  - Current Academic Year Fee Structure (itemized by authorized fee heads).
  - Annual Academic Calendar and School Managing Committee (SMC) composition and key decisions.
  - Comprehensive List of Teaching Staff including qualifications, designations, and pay scales.
  - Details of Physical Infrastructure, campus land area (in sq. meters), number of classrooms, labs, and library facilities.
  - Overall Board Examination Results and achievements for the past 3 academic years.
  - Essential Mandatory Certificates (uploaded in Hindi/English or translated notarized versions):
    1. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from State/UT Government.
    2. Recognition Certificate issued by Competent State Educational Authority.
    3. Land Certificate issued by DM/ADM/SDM/Tehsildar/Registrar (valid within 1 year).
    4. Building Safety Certificate issued by Government Works Department Officer (Assistant Engineer or above).
    5. Fire Safety Certificate issued by State/Municipal Fire Department.
    6. Safe Drinking Water and Sanitary Condition Certificate issued by Competent Government Health Authority.
    7. Society / Trust / Company Registration Certificate.
  - Annual Digital Data Upload: Mandatory annual uploading of school infrastructure, staff, and student strength data on the CBSE OASIS digital portal.
