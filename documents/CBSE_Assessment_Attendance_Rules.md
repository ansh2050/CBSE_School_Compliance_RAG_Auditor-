# CBSE Assessment Attendance Rules

## Weightage Split (Internal Assessment vs. Board Theory Examinations)
- Secondary Level Major Academic Subjects (Classes IX and X):
  - Board / Annual Theory Examination: 80 Marks (80% weightage).
  - School-Based Internal Assessment (IA): 20 Marks (20% weightage).
- Four Mandatory Pillars of 20-Mark Internal Assessment (5 Marks Each):
  - Periodic Tests (5 Marks): Written pen-and-paper tests (typically 40 marks, 1.5 hours duration); best 2 out of 3 periodic tests conducted during the academic year are averaged.
  - Multiple Assessment Strategies (5 Marks): Quizzes, oral viva, classroom debates, concept maps, exit cards, and visual expressions.
  - Portfolio & Student Work (5 Marks): Classwork submission, notebook cleanliness, student self-reflection journals, peer assessments, and creative assignments.
  - Subject Enrichment Activities (5 Marks): Science practical lab work, Mathematics lab activities, Assessment of Speaking and Listening (ASL) for English/Hindi, and Social Science project work.
- Secondary Level Skill-Based Subjects (Classes IX and X):
  - External Board / Annual Examination: 50 Marks Theory.
  - Practical Examination / School Assessment: 50 Marks Practical.
- Senior Secondary Level Subjects (Classes XI and XII):
  - Non-Practical Subjects (Mathematics, Commerce, Humanities): 80 Marks Theory : 20 Marks Internal Assessment.
  - Practical-Based Science / IT Subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, Geography): 70 Marks Theory : 30 Marks Practical Examination (or 60/40, 50/50 depending on specific subject syllabus scheme).

## Passing Criteria and Minimum Percentage Requirements
- Class IX and Class X Passing Mandate:
  - A candidate must secure a minimum of 33% marks separately in the External Board/Annual Theory Examination (i.e., 26 out of 80 marks) and 33% marks in Internal Assessment/Practical (i.e., 7 out of 20 marks), achieving an overall aggregate of 33% in each subject.
- Class XI and Class XII Passing Mandate:
  - A candidate must secure a minimum of 33% marks in Theory AND 33% marks in Practical separately, in addition to achieving 33% marks in aggregate in each subject to be declared pass.
- School-Based Internal Co-Scholastic Grading:
  - Co-scholastic areas (Art Education, Health & Physical Education, General Studies) are evaluated on an 8-point grading scale (A1 to E) or 5-point scale (A+ to C) without numerical marks upscaling.

## Minimum Attendance Percentage Requirements
- Absolute Minimum Attendance Threshold: Minimum 75% attendance of total working days in an academic session is compulsory for Classes IX, X, XI, and XII to be eligible to appear for Board / Annual Examinations (Clause 6.5 and Clause 13.1(i) of Examination Bye-Laws).
- Shortfall & Condonation Rules:
  - Shortfall in attendance below 75% renders the candidate ineligible for Board examinations.
  - Shortfall up to 15% (i.e., attendance between 60% and 74%) may be condoned by the Chairman, CBSE, strictly on valid medical grounds (supported by registered medical certificates), representation in National/International Sports events, or extreme family bereavement.

## Grace Marks Algorithms and Compartment Examination Rules
- Compartment Category Rules for Class IX and Class XI:
  - A student failing in 1 out of 5 core subjects is placed in the Compartment category and granted 1 chance to clear the compartment examination by securing at least 33% pass marks in that subject.
- Compartment Category Rules for Class X and Class XII Board Examinations:
  - Class X: A candidate failing in up to 2 subjects is placed in the Compartment category.
  - Class XII: A candidate failing in 1 subject is placed in the Compartment category.
- Replacement Rule via 6th Additional Subject:
  - If a candidate opts for a 6th additional subject and fails in 1 of the compulsory core subjects (Mathematics, Science, or Social Science in Class X), the failed core subject is replaced by the passed 6th subject for passing result calculation, provided the student clears 2 compulsory languages. The student retains the option to clear the failed core subject in the Compartment exam.
- Grace Marks and Result Moderation:
  - Board algorithms apply grace marks / score moderation rules under standard board evaluation policy to candidates falling short by marginal marks to reach the 33% passing threshold in individual theory papers.
