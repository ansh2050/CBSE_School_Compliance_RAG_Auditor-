User Upload Verification Matrix
User Persona Context
This application supports two distinct user personas. The UI must provide a "User Role" toggle (Parent/Guardian vs. School Administrator) to determine which document types are accepted and how baseline rules are applied.

Parent/Guardian: Can upload fee receipts, PDC demands, admission letters, TC withholding notices, and report cards.
School Administrator: Can upload infrastructure certificates, staff lists, library/computer lab registers, and public disclosure reports.
Acceptable User Documents for Compliance Verification
Parent/Guardian Documents
Fee Receipts and Payment Invoices: Verified against authorized fee heads, capitation fee prohibitions, and PDC collection bans. (Note: Exact fee amounts are governed by State Laws; baseline provides CBSE overarching rules).
School Prospectus and Admission Information Brochures: Verified against fee structure declarations, non-profit mandates, and prohibited screening procedures.
Formal Requests or Demands for Post-Dated Cheques (PDCs): Verified against the strict ban on PDC collections (CBSE Circular No. CBSE/CO/112793/2020).
Transfer Certificates (TCs) and Application Receipts: Verified against rules prohibiting withholding of original student documents under financial disputes.
Report Cards and Assessment Schemes: Verified against 80/20 mark split, 33% passing criteria, and internal assessment pillars.
School Administrator Documents
Fee Structure Circulars and SMC Resolutions: Verified against State Education Department approval norms and arbitrary fee hike regulations.
Land Certificates (DM/SDM/Tehsildar Formats): Verified against campus acreage, ownership norms, and 1-year issue validity requirements.
Building Safety Certificates (Govt. Assistant Engineer Rank or Above): Verified against structural integrity and spatial classroom/lab dimensions (500 sq ft minimums).
Fire Safety Certificates (State/Municipal Fire Dept): Verified against fire safety clearance and validity period.
Potable Water and Sanitation Certificates: Verified against student-to-toilet ratios (1:40 - State RTE Rule, not CBSE), female sanitary infrastructure, and water purity norms.
State No Objection Certificates (NOC) and Recognition Certificates: Verified against RTE compliance and state affiliation authorization.
Official Staff and Teacher Qualification Lists (OASIS Data Reports): Verified against Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR 1:30), NCTE teacher qualifications, and librarian credentials.
School Library Stock Registers and Audit Ledgers: Verified against minimum 1,500 book count, 5 books per student scaling ratio, and newspaper/periodical mandates.
Computer Laboratory Stock Registers and Invoices: Verified against 1:20 / 1:18 computer-to-student ratios, server specifications, and 20-25 workstation baselines.
Unacceptable/User Documents OUT OF SCOPE
Daily Class Timetables and Weekly Teacher Period Rosters: Operational scheduling documents that cannot be verified against statutory affiliation regulations.
Student Homework Assignments and Notebook Submissions: Individual academic tasks out of scope for regulatory compliance verification.
Internal Class Test Question Papers and Answer Sheets: Internal formative evaluations out of scope for statutory audit.
Individual Teacher Behavior and Conduct Complaints: Personal HR/administrative disputes handled internally by SMC or local authorities, out of scope for board compliance audit.
Student Disciplinary Notes and Detention Records: Internal school disciplinary actions out of scope for affiliation compliance.
Canteen and Cafeteria Food Menus: Internal facility offerings not governed by CBSE affiliation regulations.
Co-curricular Activity Event Photos and Internal Cultural Flyers: Non-regulatory promotional materials out of scope for legal compliance.
PTA Meeting Minutes on Non-Fee Administrative Topics: Routine parent-teacher discussions not involving statutory compliance mandates.
